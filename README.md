# HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Excel, Power Query, and Power BI**.
The objective of the project is to analyze employee workforce data and identify patterns and trends related to **employee attrition, age, gender, department, job role, education, business travel, marital status, and job satisfaction**.
The dashboard transforms HR data into an interactive reporting solution that can help HR teams and management understand workforce composition and identify areas associated with higher employee attrition.
---
## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall employee workforce and attrition.
* Calculate and monitor the employee attrition rate.
* Identify attrition patterns across different age groups.
* Analyze attrition by department and education field.
* Identify job roles with higher attrition rates.
* Understand employee job satisfaction levels.
* Analyze workforce characteristics using interactive filters.
* Present HR insights through an easy-to-use Power BI dashboard.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard provides analysis across multiple HR dimensions:

### Employee Demographics

* Employee distribution by age group
* Gender-wise employee distribution
* Average employee age

### Employee Attrition

* Total employee attrition
* Overall attrition rate
* Attrition by age
* Attrition by department
* Attrition by job role
* Attrition by education field

### Job Satisfaction

The dashboard provides a job satisfaction analysis by job role using ratings from **1 to 4**, allowing comparison of satisfaction levels across different roles.

### Workforce Filters

Interactive slicers allow users to analyze the dashboard based on:

* Gender
* Marital Status
* Business Travel
* Education
